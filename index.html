Star feature · Keyboard

Type the way you do on your phone.

Typing a search with the D-pad means hunting for one letter at a time. Here, the phone opens its keyboard on its own when a text box gets focus on the TV. The whole text is sent each time, so capitals, autocorrect, paste and any character just work.

  • Fast typing stays in step with the TV
  • Back, Home and the pad stay on screen
  • Falls back to plain key presses on TVs that need it
More features

And the rest of the remote

Built for the way people really use a TV: quick taps, long scrolls, and talking when your hands are full.

Star feature

Voice, two ways

Send your phone's microphone to the TV's own assistant, or say a command to the phone itself, like "volume up", "open Netflix" or "search lofi music".

Touchpad mode

Swap the D-pad for a touch surface. Swipe to send arrow keys (a longer swipe sends more) and tap to select.

Star feature

Type on your phone

Typing a search or a password on the TV's on-screen keyboard is slow. Use your phone's keyboard instead, with autocorrect, paste and capital letters.

Zoom-style volume bar

Drag the thumb and the TV follows. The bar shows the TV's real volume, and your phone's volume buttons control the TV too.

App shortcuts

One tap opens YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar or Spotify on the TV.

Finds your TV itself

The app remembers your TV's address and looks for it on the Wi-Fi again if the address changes. Tap the status line to pick a TV or type in an IP address.

Power, Back, Home, media

Power, back, home, play and pause, skip, rewind, mute and subtitles are all one tap away.

Private by design

Your phone talks to your TV over your own network, using TLS. The pairing key is encrypted on your phone.

How it works

Pairing takes three steps

1

Same Wi-Fi

Connect your phone and TV to the same Wi-Fi network, then open the app.

2

Show the code

Tap Show code on TV. A 6-character code appears on the screen.

A7K9QZ
3

Type it in

Enter the code on your phone. You only do this once. The dot turns green and you're in control.

Privacy and security

Your remote talks to your TV, not to us.

The app uses the same open protocol as Google's Android TV Remote, over TLS with a pinned certificate. There is no account, no server of ours in the middle and no ads. Voice in Phone mode is handled by your phone's own speech service.

  • The pairing key is encrypted with the Android Keystore
  • Left out of backups, and plain HTTP is blocked
  • The microphone is used only after you tap the mic
FAQ

Good to know

Which TVs does it work with?

Any Android TV or Google TV that supports the Android TV Remote service, not only Xiaomi. That covers most TVs and streaming boxes from brands like Sony, TCL, Philips and Xiaomi. It has been tested on a Xiaomi Mi TV so far. Your phone and TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Do I need developer mode or ADB?

No. Nothing needs to be switched on in the TV's settings. You pair once with a code shown on the TV.

Does it work without internet?

Controlling the TV only needs your local Wi-Fi network. Voice in Phone mode uses your phone's speech service, and depending on your phone that may need an internet connection.

Why are there no Settings or Input buttons?

Some TVs ignore those two keys when they arrive over the network remote protocol, so the app leaves them out instead of showing buttons that do nothing.

Is this an official Xiaomi or Google app?

No. It is an independent app. "Xiaomi", "Mi", "Android TV" and "Google TV" are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe compatibility.
