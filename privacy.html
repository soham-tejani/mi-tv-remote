Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Mi TV Remote ("the app") turns your phone into a remote control for your Android TV. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app works on your own Wi-Fi network, has no accounts, shows no ads and sends nothing to servers run by us.

What the app does

The app finds your TV on your local network, pairs with it using a code shown on the TV, and then sends remote-control commands (such as arrow keys, volume and text you type) directly from your phone to your TV over an encrypted (TLS) connection. It does not need an internet connection to control the TV.

Information the app uses

  • Microphone audio. The app asks for microphone permission for the voice feature. Audio is captured only while you have tapped the mic button.
    • In TV assistant mode, your voice is streamed over your local network to your own TV, which processes it with its built-in assistant.
    • In Phone mode, your phone's speech recognizer turns your speech into a command. Depending on your phone, this may be done on the device or by the provider of your phone's speech service (for example Google). That processing is governed by that provider's privacy policy, not ours.
    • The app does not record, save or upload your audio.
  • Text you type. When you use the keyboard feature, the text is sent to your TV so it can appear in the TV's text box. The app does not store it.
  • Network information. The app uses your Wi-Fi connection and local network discovery (multicast) to find your TV, and the TV's IP address to connect to it.

Information stored on your phone

  • The pairing key that identifies your phone to your TV, protected with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • The TV's address and your in-app settings (for example whether the phone's volume buttons control the TV).

This data stays on your phone. It is excluded from Android backups. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries that send data to us or to others.
  • No selling or sharing of your personal information.
  • No data sent to servers operated by the developer.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Connections to your TV use TLS with a pinned certificate. The pairing key is encrypted at rest. Plain, unencrypted HTTP traffic is blocked. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we have designed the app to keep as little data as possible.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the microphone permission in your phone's settings. The remote still works, but voice control will not.
  • You can remove your pairing at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If your TV lists paired remotes in its settings, you can remove this phone there too.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example by adding a new feature that uses a server, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
